Concrete Canoe.

We’re a student team at NYU Tandon. Each year, we design, build, and race a concrete canoe for the ASCE Concrete Canoe Competition.

Materials

Concrete mixes, material selection, and testing.

Design & construction

Hull design, mold fabrication, casting, and finishing.

Paddling

Training and racing together at competition.

Built on a growing legacy.

Our 2026 canoe, Chrysalis, placed first overall at Regionals and third at Nationals. Each season brings a new challenge, and something to build on.
